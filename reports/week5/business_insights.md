# Business Insights

## 1. West region is the dominant revenue engine
- Finding: The West region generated the highest revenue at $34,309,749.58.
- Supporting evidence: This is 56.21% of the total revenue of $61,039,689.51.
- Business implication: The West region is the primary growth driver and should receive the highest marketing, sales, and inventory attention.
- Recommendation: Allocate more sales and service capacity to the West and monitor its performance against the rest of the network.

## 2. Laptops are the single biggest revenue driver
- Finding: The Laptop product generated $28,023,050.88 in revenue.
- Supporting evidence: That is 45.91% of all revenue across the dataset.
- Business implication: Laptop sales have a disproportionate impact on the overall business mix.
- Recommendation: Maintain strong stock availability and upsell complementary accessories around laptop sales.

## 3. Electronics is the core category and controls most of the business value
- Finding: Electronics contributed $52,680,285.38 in revenue.
- Supporting evidence: This is 86.30% of the total revenue.
- Business implication: Most profit potential and risk exposure are concentrated in one category.
- Recommendation: Use Category-level pricing and product-mix review to protect margin quality in Electronics.

## 4. Revenue peaks in June, but the business remains seasonal
- Finding: June recorded the highest monthly revenue at $6,296,528.44.
- Supporting evidence: This was the highest single-month total in the dataset and exceeded the monthly average trend.
- Business implication: Sales demand is strong in mid-year, but the business also experiences meaningful month-to-month volatility.
- Recommendation: Use demand forecasting to align staffing, promotions, and inventory before peak revenue months.

## 5. Net margin remains healthy but varies by product line
- Finding: The average net margin across transactions is 22.55%.
- Supporting evidence: Net profit totaled $13,918,375.60 on revenue of $61,039,689.51.
- Business implication: The company retains a strong operating margin, but margin quality differs by product category and product type.
- Recommendation: Continue monitoring margin-sensitive items, especially products with lower-than-average contribution.

## 6. Desk, Mouse, and Headphones are the weakest margin products
- Finding: Desk had the lowest margin at 20.70%, followed by Mouse at 20.83% and Headphones at 21.53%.
- Supporting evidence: These products fall below the portfolio average of 22.55%.
- Business implication: Low-margin products may be weighing on overall profitability despite decent sales volume.
- Recommendation: Review pricing strategy, procurement cost, or promotions for these product lines.

## 7. Order cancellations are present but manageable
- Finding: 58 transactions were cancelled, representing 6.82% of all orders.
- Supporting evidence: 58 cancelled orders out of 850 total transactions equals 6.82%.
- Business implication: A notable share of sales is lost after initiation, which may affect revenue predictability and operational planning.
- Recommendation: Review checkout friction, delivery exceptions, and inventory booking processes to reduce cancellation risk.

## 8. High-value sales are concentrated in the West region
- Finding: 35 of the 62 transactions above $200,000 in revenue came from the West region.
- Supporting evidence: The West region accounted for 56.45% of large-value transactions.
- Business implication: The West market is not only large in volume but also more concentrated in premium sales.
- Recommendation: Prioritize high-value account management and premium product stocking in the West region.

## 9. Average order size remains moderate but valuable
- Finding: The average quantity per order is 3.52 units.
- Supporting evidence: Quantity is a meaningful driver of revenue and should be monitored with transaction size and channel mix.
- Business implication: Order volume is stable, but premium products can materially increase revenue through higher-value transactions.
- Recommendation: Combine product-level revenue analysis with average quantity tracking to identify growth opportunities.

## 10. No duplicate transaction identifiers were found
- Finding: There are zero duplicate `Transaction_ID` values.
- Supporting evidence: Duplicate check result = 0.
- Business implication: The warehouse and transaction pipeline appear consistent and traceable.
- Recommendation: Keep the transaction ID as the primary audit key for future reporting and reconciliation.
